Postcard help

Keep your maker tab open while you read. Save project downloads a backup and adds it to the device shelf. Save PNG and Share PNG use the side currently shown. Print PDF follows your Output and Make settings; Print card prints the selected faces.

Photos & projects

Your photo stays on this device. Save a project to keep working later.

Photo formats

JPEG, PNG, WebP or GIF · up to 12 MiB / 24 MP. For HEIC, export a JPEG first. Animated GIFs use a still frame.

Device shelf & backups

Save adds the project and original photo here and downloads a backup. Nothing is stored before you choose Save. Browser storage can be cleared; keep your downloaded backup.

Photo treatments

Four fixed photo treatments. Original resets the effect. The card around your image stays clear.

Handwriting & dictation

Your captured handwriting renders in capitals. Edit any words before saving. On iPad, tap a text field and use the keyboard microphone. You can dictate URLs too, then check and edit them. There is no in-app microphone session. Keyboard dictation follows your device settings; photo handling stays local.

Front title

Title: up to 100 characters, automatically fitted to two lines.

Message fitting

Message: up to 800 characters. Actual fit depends on the font, words and QR. Shrinks to 8 pt minimum; shorten it if the preview reports no room.

Postal layout

Address prints in clear standard type. Leave it blank for address guide lines. Use light, stiff postal card stock and add the correct postage. QR and message stay above the 15 mm bottom clear zone.

Australia Post layout and stock guide

QR entry

Type, paste or use keyboard dictation.

QR printing

Generated on this device. Scan on your actual paper before posting; use a light paper or label behind the code. No photo is uploaded.

Saved links & people

Saved only when you choose Save link or Mark sent. Browser storage can be cleared: export backups for long-term keeping or transfer to another device.

Paper colour

Paper colour is a preview only. PNG exports are transparent outside the image and printed marks; PDFs leave those areas unprinted.

Print output

PDF uses 300-DPI artwork and exact page dimensions. Confirm bleed and colour requirements with your printer. Label printers need matching driver/media settings.

Save, share & print

Saved projects include your original photo and settings. PNGs and PDFs are separate finished copies. Print front and back as separate sheets first to check alignment.
